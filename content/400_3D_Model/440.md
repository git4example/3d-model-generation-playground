## 4.4 Texture Models (Optional)

### Overview
- **MR Paint**: Advanced texture painting and enhancement
- **Capabilities**: Adding detailed textures to generated 3D assets
- **Integration**: Works with all 3D models for texture enhancement
- **Purpose**: Final asset polishing and texture detail enhancement

### Key Features
- **Texture Painting**: Advanced texture painting capabilities
- **Detail Enhancement**: Adding fine details to generated models
- **Material Enhancement**: Improving material properties and appearance
- **Post-Processing**: Final step in the 3D generation pipeline

### Technical Specifications
- **Processing Time**: 5-15 seconds additional processing
- **GPU Requirements**: Medium GPU requirements
- **Input Compatibility**: Works with all generated 3D models
- **Output Enhancement**: Significantly improved texture quality

### Use Cases
- **Final Polish**: Last step in asset creation pipeline
- **Texture Refinement**: Adding custom details and variations
- **Material Enhancement**: Improving surface properties
- **Professional Quality**: Achieving production-ready texture quality
